# Property Images Directory

Place all your actual movie shooting house photos in this directory!

## Recommended File Names
- `hero.jpg` : Main banner / front exterior photo of the shooting house
- `living-hall.jpg` : Grand living room / hall with high ceiling
- `master-bedroom.jpg` : Master bedroom & balcony view
- `kitchen-dining.jpg` : Kitchen & dining area
- `lawn-garden.jpg` : Lawn, garden & front porch
- `terrace.jpg` : Rooftop skyline / open terrace
- `makeup-room.jpg` : AC green room & vanity makeup mirrors

You can add any number of additional photos (e.g. `compound.jpg`, `corridor.jpg`, `night-view.jpg`) and register them in `src/lib/business.ts` or `src/lib/photos.ts`.
Supported formats: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
